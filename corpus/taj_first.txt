Application procedure
First application for Scholarship Holders
(SH, SCYP and Diaspora)
Once you have your Hungarian residence permit, you are able to apply for a TAJ card.

We organize regular TAJ APPLICATION APPOINTMENTS in the Stipendium Hungaricum Student Office (Room 102 on the first floor of building R).

Please bring the following documents – PRINTED & FILLED – with you to our appointments to apply for a TAJ card.

We do not accept incomplete applications!

The following documents are needed:

This application form (you can find a guide here)
A copy of your passport (valid for at least 3 months)
A copy of your residence permit card (both sides, valid for at least 3 months)
A copy of your address card (printed version if you received a QR code from the Immigration Office) – You should write this address on the Application form and the Authorization form!
An original student status certificate with both signature and stamp, that you can get only by visiting the ASC (formally CAO) personally. It has to be issued no longer than 30 days ago. We cannot accept digital signatures extracted from Neptun.
Your Letter of Award (a document that proves that you are receiving a monthly stipend).
Authorization form: You need to fill it out, print it and sign it. You will also need two witnesses to sign it, they can be anyone you know (“tanú” means witness). We have a guide on how to fill out. 
We will be having many TAJ application appointments, please check out our calendar for dates!
